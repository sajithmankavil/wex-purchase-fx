# Risk Register

> Formal risk register for the WEX Purchase Currency Conversion Service. Each risk has a stable ID, category, description, likelihood, impact, score (L × I), current mitigation, owner, status, target phase to address, and last-review date. Likelihood and Impact scored on a 1–5 ordinal scale; Score = L × I; the matrix below classifies score into Low (1–6), Medium (7–12), High (13–19), Critical (20–25).
>
> **Conventions:**
> - **Status:** `Open`, `Mitigated`, `Accepted`, `Transferred`, `Closed`.
> - **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-14 (Phase 1).
> - Risks below the line are watch-list items not currently rated material.

## Risk-level matrix

| Score | Class |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | Low |
| 7–12 | Medium |
| 13–19 | High |
| 20–25 | Critical |
