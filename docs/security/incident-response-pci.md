## Severity model
| Severity | Definition | Response target | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Suspected or confirmed CHD/PAN/SAD exposure, CDE compromise, payment auth compromise | Immediate | Security, legal, exec, acquirer/payment brand path |
| P1 | High-risk CDE security incident without confirmed CHD exposure | TBD | TBD |

## PCI-specific actions
- Preserve evidence.
- Engage incident commander.
- Notify security/legal/compliance.
- Determine acquirer/payment brand notification path.
- Determine forensic investigation requirement.
- Isolate impacted CDE systems.
- Rotate secrets/keys where needed.
- Produce post-incident review and evidence package.
