# Kickoff Prompt: Requirements Ingestion

Use this as the first prompt after adding your requirement document.

```text
Read CLAUDE.md and docs/requirements/source-requirements.md.

Do not code.

Act as the Product Analyst agent first.

Create or update:
- docs/requirements/functional-requirements.md
- docs/requirements/non-functional-requirements.md
- docs/requirements/acceptance-criteria.md
- docs/requirements/assumptions-and-open-questions.md
- docs/requirements/traceability-matrix.md

Then return:
1. Summary of the product/system
2. P0/P1 functional requirements
3. P0/P1 non-functional requirements
4. Ambiguities and assumptions
5. Recommended milestones
6. Risks that could impact production readiness

Follow CLAUDE.md completion format.
```
